# Working concepts

| Term | Working meaning | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge | Understanding concepts, facts and methods | Reliable application |
| Skill | Learned ability to perform an identifiable activity | Competence in every context |
| Competency | A context- or role-specific bundle of knowledge, skills and behaviours | A universal psychological quantity |
| Capability | Ability to achieve a relevant result under stated conditions | That organisational capability is the sum of individual scores |
| Expertise | Domain-specific judgement, including exceptions and limits | That tenure alone proves it |
| Adaptive expertise | Using established practice and revising it for unfamiliar problems | A generic adaptability score |
| Professional identity | A revisable account of one's role, commitments and belonging | A list of skills or a personality diagnosis |
| Routine | A recurring pattern of coordinated activity | That a documented process describes actual work |

Sources: R1 §§1–2; R2 §2. These are useful analytical conventions; disciplinary meanings differ. OE is better treated here as a field of practice with multiple capabilities than as one assessable skill.
