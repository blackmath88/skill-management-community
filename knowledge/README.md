## Evidence discipline

Use: verified public-source statement; participant note; report-derived claim; editorial interpretation; proposal; unknown. Record source, date, inspected scope and limitations. Earlier public checks are documented in the source register; they are not verification of every citation.

Promote useful research into a topic note; link a practical application into the [workbench](../workbench/README.md). Keep original-source provenance and public-copy redactions explicit.
