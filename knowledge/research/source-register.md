## Analysis of source quality

Strengths: the reports distinguish provider claims from research, identify several abstract-only readings and distinguish learning from performance. R1 makes the organisational level explicit; R2 avoids treating the avatar as an assessment.

Limitations and corrections to carry forward:

1. Both exports contain hidden citation spans and long tails of references. A footnote's presence is not evidence that it supported the visible argument. The catalogue flags visible citations separately from hidden-only / uncited entries.
2. R1 fn120 is `communication.team_personas`, not a public bibliographic source. It is preserved and flagged, not accepted as evidence.
3. Perplexity conversation links, third-party aggregators, generic homepages and unrelated-looking entries are search leads only. R2 fn123 (a company-officer registry URL) has no established relevance here. Do not harvest its personal information.
4. R2 gives a precise job title and start date for Daniel Strütt. This curation only establishes his event-contact role from the supplied invitation; the career details remain unverified.
5. R2 uses “Miro” in one personalised example, while the invitation addresses Achim. Treat this as source-text inconsistency; do not derive a second participant or personal identity claim from it.
6. R2 distinguishes journal and working-paper samples for The Cybernetic Teammate. Those publication/version claims have not been rechecked here; retain them as a verification task, not a settled headline statistic.
7. Reported effect sizes for coaching, feedback, debriefing and gamification are not reused as universal forecasts. Populations, outcomes, publication versions and methods need checking before external reuse.
8. The broad repeated claim that only 12% apply training is not accepted as a universal transfer rate. The underlying primary evidence has not been established here.
9. Report agreement does not count as independent evidence: they share sources and originate from closely related prompts.
10. Practical cases are hypothetical design exercises. They are not findings about the University of Basel, PostFinance or named community members.

## Maintenance record format

For every promoted claim: ID; exact claim; source URL / report section; source type; publication and access dates; inspected scope; population and setting; limitations; status; linked question or decision. When evidence changes, supersede the interpretation while retaining its provenance.
