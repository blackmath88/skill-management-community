# Learning and transfer

Status: report-derived working synthesis, 11 November 2026.

Distinguish exposure, understanding, attempted application, observed quality, transfer into another situation and organisational outcomes. Attendance, reflection volume and assisted output are not interchangeable evidence.

The supplied reports propose linking practice, feedback, another attempt and enabling work conditions. Underlying studies vary; use the [source register](../research/source-register.md) before making effectiveness claims.
