# Leadership and learning conditions

Status: working synthesis, 11 November 2026.

Leadership support becomes observable through priority, time, authority, resources, role modelling and follow-through. This is a working hypothesis from participant notes and supplied research, not a claim that endorsement alone causes successful development.

Ask: could a capable person succeed under the same mandate, workload, incentives and access? Treat an organisational barrier separately from an individual learning need.
