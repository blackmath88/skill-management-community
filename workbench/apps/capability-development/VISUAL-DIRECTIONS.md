# Skilling Me Softly — visual directions

Date: 11 November 2026.
Status: exploration brief. The owner requested different looks and feels, including pixel art, Scandinavian/Implement-inspired, banking and industrial settings. The specific designs below are proposals; no theme or board has been built or selected.

[Companion identity and rendering brief](COMPANION.md) · [Concept](CONCEPT.md) · [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) · [Plan](PLAN.md)

## Independent choices

- Theme: typography, colour, spacing, surfaces, illustration and motion.
- Presentation mode: character-led, journey-led or evidence-led.
- Content pack: skills, situations, methods, criteria and sources.
- Governance: permissions, sharing and evidence rules; never controlled by a visual theme.

Industry does not determine appetite for play. Test preferences with people from each context. Scandinavian/Implement-inspired describes a design exploration, not an official brand, affiliation or permission to copy protected assets.

## Four proposed worlds

| World | Visual grammar | Character and progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pixel Workshop | Crisp pixel illustrations, limited palette, inventory-inspired cards; readable ordinary UI text | Small companion, equipment cards, campaign map, optional accessories |
| Nordic Studio | Spacious composition, clear sans-serif typography, restrained colour and geometric illustration | Abstract companion or evolving sculpture; practice cards and observations |
| Professional / Banking | Precise hierarchy, white or neutral surfaces, navy/graphite accents, quiet motion | Optional geometric emblem; development objectives and evidence records |
| Industrial Workshop | Technical drawing language, sturdy typography, modular panels and functional colour | Assembled object or tools; practice stations and test records |

Palette details are unselected. Preserve the existing app's current design decisions until an explicit exploration supersedes them. In particular, do not silently undo the white-canvas and sans-serif direction described in LeaderMe's visual-polish work.

## Same meaning across worlds

Use one fictional activity: verify an AI-generated claim in a briefing.

| Record | Pixel | Nordic | Professional | Industrial |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Method | Equipment card | Practice card | Working method | Tool card |
| Campaign | Quest map | Development journey | Development objective | Practice sequence |
| Evidence | Field notes | Observations | Evidence record | Test record |
| Optional cosmetic reward | Accessory | Sculptural detail | Milestone motif | Assembly detail |

Always retain an explanation of the underlying record. “Test record” is not formal certification; a badge is not verified competence. Theme changes never alter eligibility, privacy, saved evidence or history.

Proposed identity experiment: retain a recognisable face and silhouette across a pixel sprite, geometric sculpture, line illustration and industrial assembly. Switching style should not erase chosen identity or user-curated memories. Identity is available from day one; the companion prototype has no earned appearance. Exact compatibility and retention rules remain open.

## Proposed next design deliverable

Longer-term exploration: four comparable art-direction boards, each showing:
- Home with the next useful action.
- The same practice activity.
- Review with observation, interpretation and next move.
- Character, typography, palette, controls, empty/error/save states and mobile layout.

Immediate sequence after the research review: first show three compositions of one Nordic/Assemblage family, then translate the same identity into pixel and precision styles. See [visual references](VISUAL-REFERENCES.md). Banking and industrial directions need contextual feedback; avoid assuming visual stereotypes are user needs.

Use equivalent content and records across variants. Compare clarity, willingness to use, perceived judgement, distinction between XP and evidence, and ease of finding the next action. Enjoyment alone is insufficient.

## Accessibility and trust requirements

Keep text readable; pixel styling belongs primarily in illustration. All variants need keyboard operation, visible focus, adequate contrast, non-colour state cues, reduced motion and a calm static option. Essential actions must remain available without the character.

Themes are reviewed assets and configuration, not arbitrary executable third-party plugins. Renderers receive only the necessary presentation model, never the journal. A branded appearance must not imply employer access, endorsement or assessment authority.

## Direction clarified — 12 November 2026

The owner selected a modular non-human buddy/alter ego as the product centre. All visual worlds should preserve a recognisable chosen identity and inspectable history; no human avatar, personality diagnosis or punishment for absence. The character-free presentation is an accessibility/choice alternative, not a reason to reduce the main experience to a dashboard. Start from the existing Zdog direction; investigate a richer renderer only if the visual proof exposes a concrete limitation. The research review and [adopted prototype boundaries](RESEARCH-DECISIONS.md) supersede earlier suggestions to build two engines or earned cosmetics in the first slice.
