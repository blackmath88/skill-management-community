# Trend-radar pilot

Status: idea; not scheduled or run. Date: 11 November 2026.

Question: does connecting external signals to specific work produce a more useful target capability picture?
Hypothesis: task-level translation helps discard irrelevant trends and identify actionable capability or work-design needs.

Scope: one organisational priority, three sourced signals, one consenting team; use synthetic or explicitly cleared examples. Complete the [worksheet](../tools/trend-radar-worksheet.md), then ask a peer to challenge each mapping.

Record: which decisions changed, ambiguous categories, evidence gaps, time required, disagreements and organisational barriers. A small pilot can establish usability, not general effectiveness.

Stop or revise if no concrete decision emerges, maintenance burden exceeds value, or safe case sharing is impossible. Retain only de-identified lessons approved for sharing; discard temporary person mappings.

Result: not available. Next decision: agree a suitable case and local participation before starting.
