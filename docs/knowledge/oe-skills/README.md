## How to use this collection

Read the synthesis, bring one real decision to the community, choose one bounded experiment, and return with an observation that could change the original interpretation. Record structural barriers separately from individual learning needs.

For future updates, record date, source, supported claim, scope, uncertainty and next question. Preserve disagreement and superseded interpretations. A report saying it verified a source is not the same as this repository independently checking it.

Evidence labels used throughout:

- **Verified public source:** a relevant public page was inspected on 11 November 2026; this verifies what the source states, not effectiveness.
- **Participant note:** the owner's account in this conversation; paraphrased, not a transcript or an official organisational position.
- **Report-derived:** supplied research synthesis; primary source not independently checked here unless recorded otherwise.
- **Proposal:** an interpretation, question or possible experiment; not an accepted product requirement or authority to execute.
- **Unknown:** insufficient evidence; do not complete gaps by inference.

English curation follows repository convention. The German reports are retained as source artefacts. Conversation links are explicitly removed for this public copy; original-source digests remain in the catalogue. Their references include search residue and unverified entries; use the evidence register before reusing claims.

## Relationship to Leader Me

Leader Me is a related, separate application: [character concept](https://github.com/blackmath88/leader-me/blob/main/docs/CHARACTER-STUDIO.md) and [profile model](https://github.com/blackmath88/leader-me/blob/main/docs/PROFILE.md) (private repository; access required). This research suggests keeping practice history, current development hypotheses and organisational conditions distinct. It does not change existing progression, authorise XP rules, introduce AI calls or initiate a community pilot.

## Maintenance boundaries

Keep public professional facts and owner-authorised event notes here. Keep personal journals, private peer cases, assessments and identifiable feedback out of git. Do not store invitation tracking links, unsubscribe tokens or private photo links. Sharing a curated question with peers does not imply sharing a personal profile.

No autonomous monitoring, participant outreach or scheduled work is configured by this collection.
